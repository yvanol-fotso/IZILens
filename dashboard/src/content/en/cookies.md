## What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We also use similar technologies (local storage).

## Categories

- **Strictly necessary**: sign-in session, security, chosen language and storing your consent. Always active.
- **Functional**: optional preferences, such as the display theme.
- **Analytics** and **performance**: measurement of site use and performance. *No tool is currently deployed.*
- **Advertising and tracking**: *no advertising technology is currently deployed.*
- **Unclassified**: technologies under review.

No optional cookie is loaded before you consent.

## Manage your choice

You can accept, reject optional cookies or customise your choices, then change or withdraw them at any time with the button below or the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer. Your choice is kept for six months and requested again if this policy changes.

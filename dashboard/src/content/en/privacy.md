## Who we are

IZI LENS is a Kafintech Group solution located at 305 - 14903 93 Ave NW, Edmonton, Alberta T5R 5H1, Canada. For any question about your personal data, write to **contact@izilens.com**.

## Data we process

- **Account data**: name, email address, role, organization, sign-in logs.
- **Verification data**: identity document images, OCR-extracted data, check results and review decisions. This data is processed on behalf of the customer organization requesting the verification.
- **Technical data**: IP address, request identifier and security logs.
- **Browsing data**: only with your consent where consent is required (see the Cookie Policy).

## Why we process it

To provide and secure the service, verify identities at our customers' request, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations and improve the product. For verifications, the customer organization determines the purpose; IZI LENS acts as a processor.
